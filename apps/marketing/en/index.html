What goes wrong daily

Three costs nobody puts a number on

Most 10–30 room properties still run availability on a spreadsheet, a paper diary and one person's memory. It works, right up until the day it doesn't.

Sold twice, updated too late

A guest books through an OTA at 2am. By the time somebody lowers the count on the other three channels it is morning, the last room has been sold twice, and the person who has to tell the guest is standing at the front desk.

Rates edited one channel at a time

Changing Songkran pricing means editing every channel, every date, every room type. Miss one and you sell a peak night at low-season rates to a fully booked city.

Numbers that arrive at month end

Occupancy, the rate you actually achieved, how many nights are already on the books for next month. If answering needs a spreadsheet, the answer arrives after the decision it was needed for.

Built for properties in Thailand — a hotel night is a calendar date in the property's own timezone, never a UTC timestamp

Why trust this

We have no client list to show you, because we are new

DeeHub is taking its first partner properties. In place of somebody else's testimonial, here is how the system is built — all of it verifiable during a trial.

Double-selling is refused by the database

The guarantee lives in a database constraint, not in screen-level code. Two people booking the last room in the same instant produces one booking and one refusal.

The price is frozen at the moment of booking

What the guest saw is stored with their booking. Changing your rates later never reaches back into a booking already taken, and a folio cannot disagree with what was agreed.

Every change has a name on it

Rate edits, cancellations, permission changes — each recorded with who did it and when, readable from the dashboard without asking anyone to open a database.

Thai from day one

Thai throughout, Thai baht, and hotel nights counted in the property's own timezone — the last detail is where foreign systems most often slip, and it distorts a whole month of room-night figures when they do.
